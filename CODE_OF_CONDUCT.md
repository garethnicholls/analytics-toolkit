# Code of Conduct

We are committed to a respectful, inclusive, and harassment-free community. Be considerate, assume good intent, give actionable feedback, and focus discussion on the work and its impact.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sustained disruption, publishing private information, and conduct that would reasonably make others feel unsafe are not acceptable. Maintainers may remove contributions or interactions that violate this standard. Report concerns privately to repository maintainers.
